# Useless Stats

## A RuneScape 3 plugin
Keep track of all the useless things you never knew you wanted to keep tabs on!

The following stats are currently being tracked:
<table>
<tr><td>Abilities used</td><td>Bank opened</td><td>Bank presets loaded</td><td>Benches sat on</td><td>Broadcasts triggered</td><td>Cabbages picked</td></tr>
<tr><td>Can't reach something</td><td>Crystal chests opened</td><td>Deaths</td><td>Dungeons finished</td><td>Elven ritual shard used</td><td>Excalibur used</td></tr>
<tr><td>Familiars/pets summoned</td><td>Fish fished</td><td>Food cooked</td><td>Food eaten</td><td>Implings caught</td><td>Items disassembled</td></tr>
<tr><td>Items dropped</td><td>Logs burned</td><td>Logs cut</td><td>Nothing interesting happens</td><td>Pickpockets</td><td>Potions drunk</td></tr>
<tr><td>Potions made</td><td>Prayers activated</td><td>Seren spirits summoned</td><td>Sheep sheared</td><td>Surged one tile</td><td>Things examined</td></tr>
<tr><td>Times rested</td><td>Times Surged</td><td>Total clue value</td><td>Total death costs</td><td>War's Retreat teleports</td><td></td></tr>
</table>
<p>Stats can be sorted alphabetically or numerically, and you can erase the data if you desire! Why would you? Well, maybe you wanna know how lucky you are with seren spirits today, or you're racing your friends to see who can finish the most amount of Dungeoneering floors?
Though who are we kidding, we know you wanna know how often you've surged one tile. Don't worry, nobody's judging you!
</p>
